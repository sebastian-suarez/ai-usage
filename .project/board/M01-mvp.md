First usable version of AI Usage: a macOS menu bar app that keeps an eye on the
usage limits of your AI subscriptions. The menu bar item shows the percentage
used of whichever limit you select; clicking it opens a panel with every limit
of that provider. Supports Claude and ChatGPT subscriptions only.
